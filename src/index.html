    About MultiGo Neo
    
      
    
    
    
    
      
    MultiGo Neo 1.1.7
    
      
    A Go record editor and player modelled on the MultiGo workspace: a 13×13 board,
         game information, a move list, and a status bar in a single-window desktop shell.
