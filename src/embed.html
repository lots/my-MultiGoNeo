MultiGo Neo — zero-JS embeds

Three boards, no per-widget JavaScript. Every <div data-multigo> is discovered and initialised by embed.js; appearance comes from the theme stylesheets and the options are plain data-* attributes.

Diagram · 9×9, coordinates, no controls

Viewer · 9×9 record, path=19, variation badges

Dark theme · 13×13 branch point

Both continuations are drawn as translucent stones labelled A and B at the branch point. Use the arrows, the mouse wheel or the keyboard when the board has focus.

Markup used above

data-sgf inline SGF · data-path position path · data-coords (optionally 1-1) · data-theme · data-variations · data-controls="false" · data-width.
